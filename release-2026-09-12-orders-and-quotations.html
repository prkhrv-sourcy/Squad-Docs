S–Quad / Team update

Release notes · 12 September 2026

Less re-entry.
Clearer quotes and orders.

Manage orders in Squad, reuse sample details and compare delivery options more easily.

Merged into main · release #184
01

Manage orders
in Squad

  • Create, view, edit and cancel orders from the Orders screen or a request’s Orders tab.
  • Keep several items in one order, each with its own supplier and quantity, plus a shared destination.
  • See sample and bulk orders, notes and progress. Conflicting edits prompt you to refresh.
02

Enter sample
details once

Sample price and details carry into the sample quotation. Sample quantity and packing stay separate from bulk.

  1. Enter sample details
  2. Create sample quotation
  3. Review the saved details
03

Clearer product
quotations

  • Record prices by quantity, add-on costs and one-time fees separately.
  • Show the final product specifications and choose which customer documents to include.
  • Get clearer warnings for missing prices and packing details.
04

Compare delivery
choices

  • Compare multiple delivery options, each with its own cost and timing.
  • Choose single-unit or carton packing separately for sample and bulk shipping estimates.
  • Quote timing estimates account for China weekends and holidays.
05

Easier customer
and address setup

Fixed company creation and addresses being mistaken for city names. Changing your city search clears the old selection.

  1. Select a city
    or add a missing one
  2. Enter the street address
  3. Save and use the address
06

Clearer supplier
messages

Attachment sending works with the updated supplier messaging service. Empty messages are hidden; unavailable older files show a clear resend message.

Before sending a quote

  • Confirm the customer’s delivery choice and add it to the order notes before invoicing. The customer dashboard needs its own update to show every delivery option.
  • Upload only documents safe to share publicly. “Include on quotation” controls whether a file appears on the quote, not who can open its link.

Scope: changes in release #184, including #179–#183 and review fixes.
