1. What this feature is for

Use this when a supplier sends a price list, invoice or other product document, especially when it contains many items. Squad extracts the products so Growth can review them together in a familiar spreadsheet.

In Google Sheets

Edit names, notes, dimensions, quantities, supplier prices and sample details. Tick Add to quote for each item the customer should receive in the quotation.

In Squad

Import the edits, review and validate products, select the customer currency and markup in the quote builder, configure delivery or adjustments in the quote builder and publish. Publishing updates the sheet prices automatically.

Three words to know
SR = sourcing request: the record containing the customer and products.
Sync = copy your sheet edits into Squad.
Validate = review an item in Squad and confirm its details are ready for quoting.

Before you start: sign into Squad and Google with accounts that can access the request and its linked workbook. For practice, use a test customer and a test SR in dev Squad. This is a guide to the simplified implementation; the checklist below is a test plan, not a claim that a live test has been completed.

2. Your first quote, step by step

  1. Upload
  2. Open sheet
  3. Edit & select
  4. Sync
  5. Validate
  6. Set quote pricing
  7. Review & publish

  1. Create a request from a supplier document

    Open the new-request page. In Work in Google Sheets, click Upload vendor documents. Select your file. Supported choices include PDF, PNG/JPG/WebP images, CSV, text and XLSX. You can upload up to 5 files: at most 10 MB each and 25 MB combined.

    Wait for extraction. Open Review extracted rows and anomalies and check names, quantities, prices and currencies against the original document. Fill the required customer/request fields and click Create SR. Corrections to extracted rows are made after creation. This vendor-import path does not start automated sourcing or supplier outreach.

  2. Open the linked Products sheet

    Open the SR’s product-options area and find Work in Google Sheets. Wait for Workbook ready, then click Open Google Sheet.

    For an existing SR without a sheet, use Review in Google Sheet. For an older multi-tab sheet, first sync existing edits, then use Switch to one product tab. This creates a new simpler workbook and keeps the original; use the new link from Squad afterward.

  3. Correct the products and choose what to quote

    Work in Products. Enter supplier prices as numbers, select their currencies, and check quantities. Tick Add to quote only for the items you want included. Check the initial selections rather than assuming every row is selected.

    Leave unused items in the sheet and untick them. Do not delete rows, paste new product rows, rename column headers, edit hidden IDs or replace formulas. Add or change the underlying products in Squad if needed, rather than inventing sheet rows.

  4. Bring sheet edits back into Squad

    Wait for Google Sheets to save. Return to Squad and click Refresh sheet changes. Open Review changes to inspect the differences. Then click Sync sheet changes.

    Refresh is not sync. While the request page is open, Squad checks for changes roughly every 30 seconds. This shows what changed; you still need to sync to import it.

    Look for Imported … cell changes. … items need validation in Squad. If Squad lists issues, fix them before continuing.

  5. Review and validate the changed items

    In Squad, review each selected item’s pricing, product details and required packing/delivery information. Complete its validation. Imported product edits clear previous validation so a changed price is not silently approved.

    The sheet intentionally has limited columns. Complete information such as carton weight, units per carton, delivery setup or other quote requirements in Squad when requested.

  6. Set pricing once, in the quote builder

    Open the quote builder after validating your selected products. Choose delivery in its shipping step, product markup in its margin step, and Quote currency and the catalog discount in its terms step.

    For optional tax, fees or extra discounts, expand Tax and extra adjustments. Enter amounts in the quote currency and click Apply adjustments. Use Products only — exclude delivery charges only when no delivery should be charged. Review adjustment amounts again if you change currency.

    The request page no longer has a separate pricing form. Google Sheets stays focused on product inputs; customer prices and the summary are calculated outputs.

  7. Check the numbers before publishing

    Review selected products, quantities, currency, delivery, fees and total in the quote builder. Publishing automatically refreshes calculated prices and writes them to the linked sheet. You do not need a separate Update prices action.

    If refreshed prices differ from the preview, Squad asks you to review the updated quotation and publish again. If the sheet update fails, publication stops; correct the issue and retry. After sheet edits, repeat refresh → sync → review/validate → quote builder. A changing sheet formula does not mean the Squad draft is up to date.

  8. Create the customer quotation in Squad

    Open the quote builder and review the customer-facing preview. Check that only the intended items are present. Use the publishing controls when the quote is ready. Publishing creates a version of the quotation; it does not automatically send it to the customer. Download or send it through your normal approved process.

4. Understand the price without a spreadsheet course

Supplier currency vs customer currency

The supplier might charge CNY 100. Keep 100 and CNY in the supplier columns. Choose USD or PHP in Squad if that is what the customer should see. Squad uses verified exchange rates to calculate the customer price.

When you change the customer currency, review all adjustment amounts too: tax 10 means 10 units of the currency currently selected, not a guaranteed equivalent of the previous currency’s amount.

Markup vs final price

A 20% markup adds 20% to the base product amount: 100 becomes 120 before delivery and other adjustments. It is not a 20% profit margin calculation.

The customer unit price can include other applicable costs configured in Squad. Use the actual calculated price when checking the line total.

Selected product total + source fees + tax + additional fees − discounts = customer total

For a normal quote, each selected product’s line total is its rounded customer unit price multiplied by quantity. Unchecked rows contribute zero. The summary is below the product rows around columns M–N.

Summary labelMeaning
Quote currency / Product markupYour current pricing choices from Squad.
Selected products / Selected samplesSum of selected line totals. Any delivery already allocated to unit prices is included here.
Source feesApplicable fees already attached to selected quote items; do not enter them again as additional customer fees.
TaxAn absolute amount added to the quote. Enter 10 for a tax amount of 10, not for a 10% tax rate.
Additional feesAn extra customer charge for the whole quote, entered in Squad.
DiscountA deduction. Enter the additional discount as a positive amount in Squad; the summary shows the subtraction. Existing applicable quote discounts can also affect the calculated total.
Customer totalThe final calculated customer amount. Do not type a replacement total into the sheet.
Why a “products” breakdown can differ from DDP line totals: DDP line prices include delivery. Products excluding delivery of $1,460 plus delivery of $620 equals $2,080 in DDP line totals. Add $93.87 one-time fees and the final amount is $2,173.87. Do not add the $620 again to the $2,080.

6. If something does not look right

What you seeWhat to do
I changed the sheet but Squad still shows the old value.Wait for Sheets to save. Click Refresh sheet changes, inspect the difference, then Sync sheet changes. Refresh alone does not import.
A product is missing from the quotation.Check Add to quote, sync the selection, validate the item and review the quote builder preview and publish on the test SR to update the sheet. Also verify that you are looking at the latest quotation version.
The customer price is blank or old.Sync product edits and review pricing in the quote builder. Publishing updates the sheet automatically. Until then, use the builder preview for current pricing.
“Prices refreshed” or a sheet update failed.Review refreshed prices in the quote builder, or fix the reported sheet issue, then retry publishing. A failed sheet update prevents publication.
An item needs validation again.This is expected after product edits are imported. Review the changed fields in Squad and validate them again.
The same field changed in both Squad and Sheets.Decide which value is correct. Reconcile the field so both agree, then refresh and sync again. Do not repeatedly overwrite it from two places.
Missing/duplicate product rows, unknown product, or rows moved during update.Restore the original row identities/structure and refresh. Avoid sorting or editing while Squad writes prices. If a row moved during writeback, retry the update after editing has stopped.
I cannot edit customer prices or headers.Those cells are protected intentionally. Edit supplier inputs in the sheet and currency/markup in Squad.
Google says I do not have access.Use the correct Google account and ask the workbook owner/admin for access. Open the linked workbook from Squad rather than making an unrelated copy.
I see several tabs or the old “Include” heading.You are using an older workbook. Sync it first, then use Switch to one product tab in Squad.
Tax, fees or discount are missing from the quote.Expand Tax and extra adjustments in the quote builder, enter numeric amounts and click Apply adjustments. Review the preview and publish a new quote version.
Does ticking Add to quote send anything to the customer?

No. It selects an item for the draft. You still sync, validate, review pricing in the quote builder and publish in Squad. Publishing and sending are separate actions.

Can I change PHP to USD by editing Supplier currency?

Only change Supplier currency when correcting what currency the supplier’s numeric price is in. To change what the customer sees, use Quote currency in the quote builder.

Do sample details or notes change the calculation?

No. They are descriptive text. Use numeric sample fee/currency fields and the sample quote controls in Squad for sample pricing and quantity.

Can I assume a sheet edit changes an already-published quote?

No. Syncing changes the working draft. Review and publish a new quotation version when you intend to replace the customer-facing version.
